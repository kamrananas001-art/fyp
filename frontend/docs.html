Welcome to the AI Assistant developer documentation. Our platform provides a seamless way to integrate advanced intelligence into your workflow.

Introduction

AI Assistant is a state-of-the-art platform designed to bring the power of GPT-4.0 and other leading language models directly to your workspace. Whether you're a developer or a pro-user, our interface is optimized for speed and clarity.

Quick Start

npm install @nexus-ai/core

Authentication

All requests to our API require an API key. You can find your key in the Settings of your dashboard under the "Data & Keys" tab.
